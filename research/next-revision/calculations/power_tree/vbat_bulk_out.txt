=== 5. Regeneration into VBAT with the pack disconnected ===
Back-EMF at 14,300 rpm: 5.27 V, far below the 12-17.4 V pack: a coasting motor never
charges VBAT through the body diodes. Only active braking pumps energy into VBAT (the bridge
boosts): at most E^2 / (4 R) = 1.91 W per motor, 3.82 W for both, at full speed.
   76 g at 4 m/s: kinetic energy 0.64 J (motor and bridge losses ignored: an upper bound)
  100 g at 4 m/s: kinetic energy 0.83 J (motor and bridge losses ignored: an upper bound)
  130 g at 4 m/s: kinetic energy 1.07 J (motor and bridge losses ignored: an upper bound)
Net charging power with 0.6 W of logic load: 3.2 W. Time for VBAT to climb from 16.8 V:

polymer: 25TQC33MYF per bridge                        5.2ms    9.3ms   19.8ms

A stop from 4 m/s lasts hundreds of ms: no capacitor bank absorbs it. Either the firmware
stops regenerative braking (coast or low-side brake, EN = 0) when VBAT rises above the pack's
maximum, or a TVS takes the energy.

=== 6. TVS clamp on the battery input (BAT_P to GND_BAT, at J1) ===
Regen clamp current about 0.15 A at about 21 V: the TVS sits near VBR (20-22.1 V),
far below VC 29.2 V at 13.7 A; 3.2 W for a few hundred ms (0.83 J at most).
Hot plug: the TVS also holds Q6's VDS (30 V absolute maximum) below 30 V.
