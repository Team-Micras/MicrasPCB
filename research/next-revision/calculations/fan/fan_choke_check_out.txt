4. REF for the chosen motor. I_run: its running current at the highest duty the fan will use.
   I_peak = max(1.5 x I_run, (I_run + h) / 0.925), h the ripple half-amplitude at the lowest plausible
   inductance (section 1: 1.05 A for a 1S motor, 1.4 A for a 2S motor): 1.5x leaves the start-up and gust margin,
   the second term keeps the ripple peak under the bottom of the REF band (above it the limiter trims the pulse
   and the mean current falls short). VREF = I_peak x RSNS x ACL = I_peak x 0.25 V/A; R29 = R28 x VREF /
   (3.3 V - VREF), R28 4.7 kOhm, the nearest E24 value at or above. The band's top (x 1.093) stays at or below 4.8 A (L6's 20 % point):
   I_peak <= 4.39 A, so I_run <= 2.93 A (1S) or 2.66 A (2S); above it the research's discrete half-bridge applies.
