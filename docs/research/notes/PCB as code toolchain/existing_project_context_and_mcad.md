## Q1. Old PCB in /home/cosme/codes/hw_debug: EDA tool and version, files, subsystems, board size and layers, known issues, automation, history

### Takeaway
The main board was designed in **Altium Designer**, a 5-sheet hierarchical project from late 2025. It was later converted to **KiCad 10.0**: the files carry the KiCad 10 format and their DRC/ERC reports were generated by KiCad 10.0.6. It is a **4-layer, 50 x 90 mm, ~1.0 mm thick** board built around an STM32H725 with 154 footprints, all mounted on the top side. The folder is **not a git repository** and has no CI. A detailed schematic review lists 5 critical, 15 major and 19 minor defects, plus a proposed power architecture for the next revision. The encoder and Bluetooth daughterboards exist **only as Altium files**.

**Known issues / TODO**
- Critical defects: C1, MCU VBAT pin on the battery net (4.0 V abs max); C2, VCAP/VFBSMPS wiring matched no supply configuration (fixed by a jumper); C3, 3V3 back-feeds VBAT through the buck, measured up to 28.3 V; C4, the 20 V boost can start in debug modes, so the motors can run; C5, three 3V3 sources hard-paralleled with no ORing. — [schematic_review.md §1, §3.1](file:///home/cosme/codes/hw_debug/schematic_review.md)
- Major M1–M15 include: the BOOT0 capacitor to 3V3 boots into the bootloader; USB-C has no ESD protection; WS2815C is a 12 V LED used at 3.3 V; wall-emitter resistors are overstressed; the boost's forced-PWM part idles at 1.1 W; SBU powers the BLE board against the Type-C spec; the L6205 lacks local decoupling. Minor m1–m19 include net-name mismatches against the firmware (m15: `LED_Blue` in the schematic vs `LED_Red` in the firmware; Motor_R_Sense/Current_Sensor_Right swapped) and an SWD header without NRST (m7). — [schematic_review.md §3.2–3.3](file:///home/cosme/codes/hw_debug/schematic_review.md)
- Proposed next-revision power tree: high-side P-FET with slow start; TPS61178 boost with EN on an MCU GPIO and a DISDRV load disconnect; ideal diodes into a single L6981C33 buck (LDO removed); MCU VBAT pin to 3V3; battery divider into PA5. — [schematic_review.md §4](file:///home/cosme/codes/hw_debug/schematic_review.md)
- Thesis future work: move the USB-C connector (its recess interferes with cable housings); SWD+BLE debug module; custom battery pack; Maxon DCX 8M motors. — [notes_tcc.md §5, §8](file:///home/cosme/codes/hw_debug/notes_tcc.md)
- Bench status as of 2026-09-23: the 3V3 buck and the 20 V boost are validated at light load; the driver, motors, MCU, USB, battery and Q6 are untested. The first MCU died in December. — [debug_session/hw_debug/README.md](file:///home/cosme/codes/hw_debug/debug_session/hw_debug/README.md)

### Inferences
- The project has **already moved its ECAD source of truth to KiCad 10**. The mechanical repo's board import and the design reviews both read the KiCad files, not the Altium ones. A new PCB toolchain that stays on KiCad 10's file format keeps the existing MCAD pipeline working unchanged.
- The 1,869 DRC and 1,351 ERC violations are most likely Altium-import artifacts (fragmented zones, library mismatches), not real errors. A clean-sheet next revision is probably cheaper than cleaning the imported file. This was not verified violation by violation.
- The missing stackup block, the 1.0412 mm thickness from the import, and the hand-copied JLC BOM all mean that fab outputs (gerbers, CPL, BOM) need to be regenerated reproducibly in whatever toolchain comes next.

### Gaps
- The exact Altium Designer release could not be determined from the binary headers.
- No gerber/drill package was found, so I could not confirm what was actually sent to JLCPCB.
- Board dimensions for the encoder and BLE daughterboards were not extracted: they are Altium-only, and no KiCad conversion exists.

## Q2. Mechanical CAD-as-code: /home/cosme/codes/MicrasHardware

### Takeaway
MicrasHardware is a **build123d** (Python, OpenCascade) parametric chassis, managed with **uv**. It is viewed in **OCP CAD Viewer (ocp_vscode)**, exports STEP, STL, DXF, SVG and PDF, and checks design rules with pytest-wrapped tools. It has **no CI, no pre-commit and no Makefile**. A working **ECAD→MCAD** pipeline already exists: `tools/export_board.py` drives the Windows KiCad 10 `kicad-cli.exe` from WSL to produce `ref/board.step`, `board.stl` and `board_silk.svg`, and parses the `.kicad_pcb` for the outline, holes and contact zones into `ref/board_mech.json`. **Board dimensions are also duplicated by hand** in `micras/params.py`.

### Inferences
- The MCAD side is mature and code-first. The cleanest fit is a PCB toolchain whose outputs `kicad-cli` can export (STEP/STL/SVG), or a native KiCad 10 project, so `export_board.py` keeps working. A toolchain without KiCad output would mean rewriting `export_board.py` and `layout.py`.
- There are **three representations** of the same board mechanics: the KiCad Edge.Cuts and pads, the generated `board_mech.json`, and the hand-typed `params.Board`. Nothing cross-checks them. This is the main gap a "single source of truth" should close.
- The ECAD→MCAD direction depends on Windows KiCad through WSL, which blocks CI. A Linux `kicad-cli` (apt or the KiCad Docker image) would make board import runnable on GitHub Actions. I did not verify the image name or tag.

### Gaps
- No ECAD-side 3D model library policy was found beyond models embedded in the `.kicad_pcb`.
- No MCAD→ECAD generation of the outline or holes exists today. The outline and holes flow only from KiCad into build123d.

## Q3. Firmware: /home/cosme/codes/MicrasFirmware — language, MCU, build system, CI, pinout ties to the PCB

### Takeaway
The firmware is **C++23** with **CMake + CMakePresets** on an **STM32H725** (`micras_v1.ioc`; a legacy `micras_v0.ioc` targets the STM32G474RET6). It is generated headless by **STM32CubeMX 6.18.1 in Docker** and runs CI on **GitHub Actions** (build/lint/format, Doxygen docs to Pages, release). The PCB↔firmware link is the **.ioc GPIO_Label names** (70 pins) plus `config/targets/v1/target.hpp`, which maps peripheral handles. Robot geometry (outline lengths, wall-sensor positions, wheel radius, track) is **hand-entered** in `robot.hpp`. Known net-name mismatches with the schematic already exist.

### Cited Findings

## Q5. ECAD↔MCAD co-design options for build123d (2025–2026 state)

### Takeaway
**KiCad 10 (released 2026-03-20)** is the practical hub:
- **ECAD→MCAD** is solved: `kicad-cli pcb export step|glb|stl|brep|xao...`, then `build123d.import_step`. MicrasHardware already does this.
- **MCAD→ECAD** (outline, holes, slots from build123d) has no CLI path in KiCad 10. `kicad-cli` cannot import DXF. The IPC API (kicad-python) needs a running GUI in KiCad 9/10, and headless use arrives only in KiCad 11.

The realistic scriptable options are therefore:
- writing the Edge.Cuts and mounting-hole s-expressions directly (kiutils, kicad-skip, or the project's own s-expr code);
- generating a DXF with build123d `ExportDXF` for manual "Import Graphics";
- GUI-attached IPC scripts.

FreeCAD's KiCad StepUp offers bidirectional push/pull, but it adds FreeCAD to a pure-Python stack. Code-first ECADs (atopile, tscircuit) do not document mechanical/STEP integration with build123d.

### Cited Findings
**KiCad 10 status and CLI**
- KiCad 10.0.0 was released March 20, 2026 (10.0.1 RC in April). It moved the official 3D libraries to STEP and added importers (Allegro, PADS, gEDA) and design variants. — [KiCad blog 10.0.0](https://www.kicad.org/blog/2026/03/Version-10.0.0-Released/), [Hackster](https://www.hackster.io/news/what-s-new-in-kicad-10-63372efa034c), [KiCad 10.0.1 RC1](https://www.kicad.org/blog/2026/04/KiCad-Version-10.0.1-Release-Candidate-1-Available/)
- `kicad-cli pcb export step` flags:
  - Origin: `--grid-origin`, `--drill-origin`, `--user-origin`.
  - Model and component selection: `--subst-models`, `--board-only`, `--no-board-body`, `--no-components`, `--component-filter`, `--no-dnp`, `--no-unspecified`, `--variant`.
  - Copper and layers: `--include-tracks/pads/zones/inner-copper/silkscreen/soldermask`, `--net-filter`.
  - Geometry processing: `--fuse-shapes`, `--cut-vias-in-body`, `--fill-all-vias`, `--min-distance`, `--no-optimize-step`.
  - Variables: `-D` define-var.
  - Sibling subcommands: `brep`, `glb`, `stl`, `ply`, `xao`, `3dpdf`, `u3d`, `stpz` (same flags) and `vrml`.
  - Other commands: `pcb import` (altium/pads/eagle/cadstar/fabmaster/pcad/solidworks), `pcb upgrade` and `jobset run`. There is **no DXF import and no Python-script runner** in the CLI.
  - — [KiCad 10.0 CLI reference](https://docs.kicad.org/10.0/en/cli/cli.html)
- KiBot wraps the same 3D exports (BREP/GLB/STL/STEP/XAO) for CI pipelines. — [KiBot export_3d](https://kibot.readthedocs.io/en/latest/configuration/outputs/export_3d.html)

**KiCad scripting APIs**
- The SWIG `pcbnew` Python bindings are "deprecated as of KiCad 9.0", and "the current plan is to remove the SWIG bindings in KiCad 11.0". The IPC API with kicad-python is the recommended replacement. — [KiCad dev docs: PCB Python bindings](https://dev-docs.kicad.org/en/apis-and-binding/pcbnew/)
- "The IPC API in KiCad 9 and 10 only supports communication with a running instance of the KiCad GUI." It "has no support for plotting or exporting files". KiCad 9/10 support IPC plugins only in the PCB Editor. "Support for running in headless mode through kicad-cli was added for KiCad 11." — [KiCad dev docs: IPC API for add-on developers](https://dev-docs.kicad.org/en/apis-and-binding/ipc-api/for-addon-developers/)
- Forum reports say SWIG and the bundled Python are already gone in the 10.99 (v11) nightlies, with the v11 release expected around early 2027. This is unofficial. — [KiCad forum: migration schedule](https://forum.kicad.info/t/migration-schedule-from-the-old-swig-api-to-the-ipc-api/69437), [KiCad forum: 10.99 missing python.exe](https://forum.kicad.info/t/kicad-10-99-win-missing-python-exe/71638)
- kicad-python (`kipy`) exposes `Board.get_shapes()`, `get_footprints()`, `get_stackup()`, `get_origin()/set_origin()`, `get_nets()`, `get_items()`, and shape classes `BoardSegment`, `BoardArc`, `BoardCircle`, `BoardRectangle`, `BoardPolygon` with a writable `layer`. Item creation uses commit transactions (`begin_commit`). — [kicad-python Board docs](https://docs.kicad.org/kicad-python-main/board.html)
- Forum users report that drawing board outlines through the IPC API is under-documented, and that the API is not supported in the footprint editor. — [KiCad forum: IPC footprints](https://forum.kicad.info/t/using-the-ipc-to-create-footprints/67720), [KiCad forum: clarifications on IPC API](https://forum.kicad.info/t/clarifications-on-new-ipc-api/61876)
- File-level Python libraries that need no KiCad install: **kiutils** (dataclass parser for KiCad 6+ files that preserves file layout for version control) and **kicad-skip** (manipulates KiCad 7+ s-expression schematic, netlist and PCB files). Older options are pykicad and kicad-pycbnew. I did not confirm that they support KiCad 10's format. — [kicad-skip (PyPI)](https://pypi.org/project/kicad-skip), [kiutils (GitHub)](https://github.com/FlashPCB/kiutils), [pykicad (PyPI)](https://pypi.org/project/pykicad), [kicad-pycbnew (PyPI)](https://pypi.org/project/kicad-pycbnew/0.1.1/)

**build123d side**
- build123d provides `import_step()` (returns a Compound), `export_step`, `export_gltf` (binary option), `export_stl`, `export_brep`/`import_brep`, `import_svg`, `import_dxf`, `ExportDXF(version='AC1027', unit=Unit.MM, ...)` with `add_layer` and `add_shape`, and `ExportSVG`. — [build123d import/export docs](https://build123d.readthedocs.io/en/latest/import_export.html)
- KiCad cannot use enclosure STEP for the outline. The common route is to slice the MCAD model at the PCB plane, export DXF/SVG, and import it onto Edge.Cuts. DXF units are optional or absent, so dimensions must be checked after import. — [KiCad forum: PCB shape from enclosure STEP](https://forum.kicad.info/t/pcb-shape-from-enclosure-step-file/30935), [KiCad forum: case files to Edge.Cuts](https://forum.kicad.info/t/plastic-case-dwg-igs-stp-files-to-kicad-board-edge-cuts/55650)

**KiCad StepUp (FreeCAD)**
- StepUp pushes a FreeCAD sketch to the KiCad board edge (Edge.Cuts) and pulls the edge back. Sketches must stay within what KiCad can represent. — [FreeCAD wiki: KicadStepUp](https://wiki.freecad.org/KicadStepUp_Workbench)
- Reported pitfalls: placement offsets, fixed by using the grid origin for both StepUp and KiCad, and arcs flipping direction after a push (seen with StepUp 12.3.4 and KiCad 8.0.7). — [KiCad forum: StepUp sketcher](https://forum.kicad.info/t/kicad-stepup-the-sketcher-for-getting-to-blinky/7826?page=2), [KiCad forum: arcs wrong way](https://forum.kicad.info/t/kicadstepup-push-sketch-to-pcb-results-in-arcs-going-the-wrong-way/57459)

**Code-first ECAD**
- atopile: "Build electronics with code", MIT, pre-release and "under heavy development"; its layout "works natively with KiCad", and builds "update the `.kicad_pcb`". Its pages do not mention mechanical, outline or STEP integration. — [atopile docs](https://docs.atopile.io/), [atopile GitHub](https://github.com/atopile/atopile)
- tscircuit: the `tsci export` docs list circuit-json, schematic-svg, pcb-svg, readable-netlist and specctra-dsn. A docs PR proposes gltf/glb. A separate `circuit-json-to-step` package exists. A third-party note says `kicad_pcb` export lacks 3D model references. — [tsci export docs](https://docs.tscircuit.com/command-line/tsci-export), [tscircuit docs PR #341](https://github.com/tscircuit/docs/pull/341.diff), [circuit-json-to-step README](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/circuit-json-to-step@0.0.36/README.md)

### Inferences
- **ECAD→MCAD.** Keep the existing pipeline: `kicad-cli pcb export step --subst-models --grid-origin`, then `import_step`, then transform to the robot frame. Two upgrades are worth making:
  - Run `kicad-cli` on Linux or in CI instead of through WSL→Windows.
  - Consider `pcb export glb` for faster viewer loading. The STEP load takes about 25 s and 0.6 GB today. build123d has no glTF import, though; glb would serve web or three.js viewers rather than build123d checks.
  - Using `--user-origin` set to the axle midpoint would remove the hard-coded `AXLE_KX/KY` and `Pos(-67.1112, 64.1608)` transforms.
- **MCAD→ECAD** (outline, mounting holes, encoder slots, fan hole, wheel notches, sensor and connector placement):
  - Most compatible with the current stack: a Python script that writes or replaces `gr_line`/`gr_arc` on Edge.Cuts and the positions of mounting-hole and encoder-slot footprints directly in the `.kicad_pcb`. It would use kiutils or kicad-skip, or extend `export_board.py`'s own s-expr parser. It runs headless and in CI with no KiCad install. The risk is format churn across KiCad versions.
  - DXF via `ExportDXF` is simpler but manual (GUI Import Graphics), with unit pitfalls.
  - The IPC API is GUI-bound until KiCad 11, which should be a 2027 option.
  - StepUp works but brings in FreeCAD.
- **Single source of truth.** Promote board mechanics to one data file, e.g. TOML or JSON with `{value, source}` as in `robot.toml`, or keep the `micras/params.py` dataclasses and export from them. Generate:
  1. KiCad Edge.Cuts, hole and slot geometry, with a check that the board file matches;
  2. `board_mech.json` for build123d;
  3. the simulation's `robot.toml` `[chassis]` outline and board z;
  4. `robot.hpp` geometric constants that are not measured.
  
  A CI test then diffs every consumer against the source. Today's drift (`board_bottom` 1.0 vs 1.2 mm, three hole lists) shows why this is needed.
- **Pinout single source.** Compare the KiCad netlist (`kicad-cli sch export netlist`) with the `.ioc` `GPIO_Label` names in firmware CI. Alternatively, generate the label list from the schematic, or the reverse.
- If a code-first ECAD (atopile or similar) is chosen, it still ends in a `.kicad_pcb`, so the same `kicad-cli` ECAD→MCAD path and the s-expr MCAD→ECAD injection apply. I found no first-party build123d integration in either atopile or tscircuit.
